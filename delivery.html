DIGITAL DELIVERY

Digital Product Delivery Policy

FAROFF sells digital website source code and related digital products. No physical goods are shipped.

How delivery works

After a successful payment, the purchased digital product is delivered electronically using the delivery method stated on the individual product page. This may include a download link, repository access, or another electronic delivery method.

Delivery timing

For products marked as instant delivery, delivery is normally provided after payment confirmation. If manual processing is required, the expected delivery time will be communicated to the customer.

Delivery problems

If you complete payment but do not receive your purchased product, contact us at faroffwork@gmail.com with your order/payment details. We will verify the transaction and provide the applicable delivery or support.

Custom services

Custom website projects are delivered according to the scope and timeline agreed with the client before work begins.
